# Lock In AI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 25, 2026

## Overview

Lock In AI ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle. All data generated and stored by the App remains entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Data Storage

All data — including your profile information, face scan photos, scan scores, daily task progress, water intake logs, streaks, and achievements — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. No data is uploaded to any server, cloud service, or external database.

## Camera Usage

The App uses your device camera solely for the purpose of facial analysis. Photos taken within the App are processed entirely on-device using Google ML Kit's on-device face detection. Your photos are never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party. Photos remain stored only on your device's local storage.

## Notifications

The App may send local push notifications to remind you about daily tasks, water intake goals, and weekly face scans. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.

## Photo Library

If you choose to save scan photos, they are stored only on your device. The App does not access, upload, or share photos from your device's photo library with any third party.

## Third-Party Services

Lock In AI does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The App does not contain ads. No user data is shared with third parties for any purpose.

## Data Collection

We do not collect:
- Personal identification information
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Any data transmitted over the internet

## Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all App data at any time through the "Reset All Data" option in the Profile screen, or by uninstalling the App.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@lockinai.app.
